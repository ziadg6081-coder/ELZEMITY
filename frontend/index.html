CUSTOMER CARE

Shipping & payment.

How ordering works, step by step.

  1. Choose your scents

    Add your favourite fragrances to the cart and continue to checkout.

  2. Enter your delivery details

    Tell us where to deliver. The shipping fee is calculated according to your governorate.

  3. Pay the shipping fee via InstaPay

    After placing the order you’ll see the InstaPay details. Send the shipping fee, then upload the receipt or enter the reference number.

  4. We confirm your transfer

    The store owner confirms the transfer manually before your order is processed. Unpaid orders are cancelled automatically once the payment window ends.

  5. Pay for the perfumes on delivery

    The price of the perfumes is paid in cash when you receive your order (COD).
